Your first visit

Somewhere for your children
Children’s ministry runs during the 9am service. Under-3s meet in the breakout room off the café and are not supervised, so please stay with them. Preschoolers meet in the Lower Lounge and primary-aged children in Jubilee Hall, both via the entrance down the ramp.Sparkles · Bubbles · Rangers & Explorers
Newcomers Coffee
Five minutes after the service, coffee and baking are brought to the Newcomers Coffee space in the café. Meet some of our pastoral staff, meet other new folk, and ask anything.After both services
Someone to pray with you
A team prays with people after every service — generally, or about something specific. There is also a confidential prayer chain for urgent needs.Contact the church office
Sermon notes and wifi
Both are at the Welcome Desk as you come in the door, along with anything else you might need.

Live translation, in 21 languages

We provide live translation at both the 9am and 11am services.

Go to the Ezyspeech page in your phone’s browser to follow live captions and translation. There is no app to download and no code to enter. Bring your own earphones if you want the audio read-aloud feature.

For more information, ask the Translation team at the Welcome Desk.

Sermons

Our sermons help you apply the Bible’s truths to your everyday life and get to know God’s purpose for your life. We mix up the style of talks and often invite discussion and feedback during the service.

Services are recorded and posted to the church’s YouTube channel, where you can watch a Sunday service in full.
