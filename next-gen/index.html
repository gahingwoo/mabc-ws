    Children

    Sparkles
    Under 3 years old. Sparkles meet in the breakout room off the café. The children are not supervised, so please stay with them.Sundays 9am
    Bubbles
    Preschool-aged children meet in the Lower Lounge, via the entrance down the ramp.Sundays 9am · 3–4 years
    Rangers & Explorers
    Primary-aged children meet in Jubilee Hall, via the entrance down the ramp.Sundays 9am · 5–12 years
    For more information, email our Children’s Ministry Leader

    Gravity

    Our programme aims to teach the kids more about who Jesus is, to equip them to begin asking questions about their faith, and to seek answers to those questions in God’s word.

    This year the group attends Graction Camp in Auckland, from Friday 26 to Monday 28 September.

    When
    During the 9am service, in the Upper LoungeYears 7–9
    For more information, email Michael Kennerley

    Traction

    Our vision for Traction is that our teenagers would know, experience and share the love of Jesus. We seek to provide an inclusive and safe environment to grow in their identity and understanding of God, through discipleship and journeying alongside our young people.

    Friday youth group
    Dinner, small group discussion, games, music and spiritual input, every Friday during the school term.6.30–9pm · Jubilee Hall · dinner $5
    Social events
    At the beginning and end of each term, on a Friday night.
    Camps
    Graction Camp in Auckland, Friday 26 to Monday 28 September.
    For more information, email Youth Pastor Michael Kennerley

    Young Adults

    A vibrant group of young adults, roughly 17 to 30, who enjoy doing life together — coming to church, discussing life and faith, having regular socials, putting on and attending one-off events, and just hanging out.

    Depth
    Keen to go deeper in your faith by learning about Jesus? Join other young adults on Wednesday nights.7–9pm · Upper Lounge
    For more information, email Michael Kennerley
