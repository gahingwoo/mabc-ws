Mt Albert Community Pop Up

A free family event aimed at building community and supporting people. Hosted through the summer months except January, with a variety of stalls — products for sale, community initiatives, projects and organisations that support our local community.

More than a market: local Mt Albert groups raising awareness for the services near you, the Loved Whānau clothing and homewares swap, and a friendly atmosphere.

Mt Albert Toy Library

A huge range of toys, games, puzzles and equipment to hire and take home. Our non-profit toy library welcomes new members: there is an annual membership fee and a small borrowing fee per item, and toys can be borrowed for two weeks at a time.

English classes (ESOL)

Conversational English for people who have made New Zealand home, at beginner, intermediate and advanced levels. Classes run from the church café. There are no visa or residency requirements, and anyone is welcome to join.

Every term includes a field trip, and finishes with a shared lunch. There is also a WeChat group where two native speakers help students practise between classes.
